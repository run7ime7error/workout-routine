2-day Full Body

Day 1 — Full

Barbell squat

4×6–10
  1. Bar on upper back.
  2. Hips below knees.
  3. Stand.

Barbell bench press

3×5–8
  1. Unrack with straight arms, feet down.
  2. Lower to mid-chest.
  3. Press to lockout.

Barbell row

4×6–10
  1. Hinge, flat back.
  2. Pull to upper abdomen.
  3. Lower controlled.

DB overhead press

3×6–10
  1. Dumbbells at shoulders, palms forward.
  2. Press up. Pause.
  3. Lower to start.

Barbell Stiff Leg Deadlifts

3×6–10
  1. Soft knees.
  2. Hips back until hams load.
  3. Stand.

Cable lateral raise

3×12–15
  1. Low cable, handle in hand.
  2. Raise arm to parallel.
  3. Lower under control.

Incline DB curl

3×8–12
  1. Hang on an incline.
  2. Curl, palm to shoulder.
  3. Full lower.

Standing calf raise

4×8–15
  1. Heels hang.
  2. Stretch.
  3. Rise.

Day 2 — Full

Barbell Stiff Leg Deadlifts

3×6–10
  1. Soft knees.
  2. Hips back until hams load.
  3. Stand.

Incline DB press ~30°

4×6–10
  1. Lay on the incline bench, feet down, arms straight.
  2. Lower to mid-chest.
  3. Press to lockout. Bench ~30°.

Pull-ups

4×6–10
  1. Dead hang.
  2. Pull chest to bar.
  3. Lower to full hang.

Bulgarian split squat

3×8–12 / leg
  1. Rear foot on bench.
  2. Drop back knee.
  3. Drive front foot.

Hip thrust

3×8–12
  1. Shoulders on bench, bar on hips.
  2. Extend hips.
  3. Lower.

Overhead DB extension

3×10–15
  1. Dumbbell overhead, both hands.
  2. Lower behind the head, elbows in.
  3. Extend.

Leg extension

3×10–15
  1. Seat back, 90° start.
  2. Extend.
  3. Lower slow.

Hanging leg raise

3×8–15
  1. Hang.
  2. Curl pelvis, legs up.
  3. Lower quiet.
