6-day PPL

1 Push A2 Pull A3 Legs A4 Push B5 Pull B6 Legs B

Day 1 — Push A

Incline DB press ~30°

4×6–10
  1. Lay on the incline bench, feet down, arms straight.
  2. Lower to mid-chest.
  3. Press to lockout. Bench ~30°.

Barbell bench press

3×5–8
  1. Unrack with straight arms, feet down.
  2. Lower to mid-chest.
  3. Press to lockout.

DB overhead press

3×6–10
  1. Dumbbells at shoulders, palms forward.
  2. Press up. Pause.
  3. Lower to start.

Cable lateral raise

3×12–15
  1. Low cable, handle in hand.
  2. Raise arm to parallel.
  3. Lower under control.

Overhead DB extension

3×10–15
  1. Dumbbell overhead, both hands.
  2. Lower behind the head, elbows in.
  3. Extend.

Day 2 — Pull A

Pull-up

4×6–10
  1. Dead hang.
  2. Pull chest to bar.
  3. Lower to full hang.

Barbell row

4×6–10
  1. Hinge, flat back.
  2. Pull to upper abdomen.
  3. Lower controlled.

Straight-arm pulldown

3×10–15
  1. High cable, lean forward.
  2. Sweep to thighs with the shoulders.
  3. Return to stretch.

Incline DB curl

3×8–12
  1. Hang on an incline.
  2. Curl, palm to shoulder.
  3. Full lower.

Rear-delt fly

3×12–15
  1. Hinge.
  2. Raise arms to shoulder line.
  3. Lower.

Day 3 — Legs A

Barbell squat

4×6–10
  1. Bar on upper back.
  2. Hips below knees.
  3. Stand.

RDL

3×6–10
  1. Soft knees.
  2. Hips back until hams load.
  3. Stand.

Leg extension

3×10–15
  1. Seat back, 90° start.
  2. Extend.
  3. Lower slow.

Standing calf raise

4×8–15
  1. Heels hang.
  2. Stretch.
  3. Rise.

Hanging leg raise

3×8–15
  1. Hang.
  2. Curl pelvis, legs up.
  3. Lower quiet.

Day 4 — Push B

Barbell incline press

4×6–10
  1. 30–45° bench.
  2. Bar to mid-chest.
  3. Press.

Dip

3×6–10
  1. Lockout on bars.
  2. Lower.
  3. Press up.

Cable fly

3×10–15
  1. Pins at shoulder height.
  2. Hug, elbows fixed.
  3. Open to stretch.

DB lateral raise

3×12–15
  1. Palms to hips.
  2. Raise to parallel.
  3. Lower slow.

Pushdown

3×10–15
  1. Elbows pinned.
  2. Extend.
  3. Return.

Day 5 — Pull B

DB row

4×8–12
  1. Hinge, flat back.
  2. Elbow to hip.
  3. Long hang.

Lat pulldown

3×8–12
  1. Full overhead stretch.
  2. Bar to upper chest.
  3. Return long.

Barbell curl

3×8–12
  1. Elbows still.
  2. Curl to shoulders.
  3. Lower long.

Hammer curl

2–3×10–12
  1. Thumbs up.
  2. Curl to chest.
  3. Lower.

Shrug

3×8–15
  1. Bar at thighs.
  2. Shoulders straight up.
  3. Lower. No roll.

Day 6 — Legs B

Bulgarian split squat

3×8–12 / leg
  1. Rear foot on bench.
  2. Drop back knee.
  3. Drive front foot.

Leg curl

3×10–15
  1. Hips glued.
  2. Curl to glutes.
  3. Lower long.

Hip thrust

3×8–12
  1. Shoulders on bench, bar on hips.
  2. Extend hips.
  3. Lower.

Seated calf

3×10–15
  1. Heels hang.
  2. Stretch.
  3. Rise.

Pallof press

3×8–12 / side
  1. Side-on to cable.
  2. Press out, no twist.
  3. Return.
